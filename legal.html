Allergen notice

Please tell us about any food allergy before you order, every time, including when you order online or through a delivery app.

Our kitchen prepares food that contains or may contain these major allergens:

  • Peanuts (kung pao dishes)
  • Tree nuts (cashew and almond dishes)
  • Shellfish (shrimp, scallops, crawfish and crabmeat dishes, shrimp rolls and crab rangoon)
  • Eggs (egg rolls, egg drop soup, fried rice and egg foo young)
  • Milk (cream cheese in crab rangoon)
  • Wheat and soy (noodles, wrappers, batters and most sauces)
  • Sesame (sesame chicken, sesame shrimp and sesame balls)
  • Other allergens may be used in sauces, marinades and fried foods. Ask us about any specific dish.

All our food is made in one shared kitchen, using shared woks, equipment and fryers. Because of this, we cannot guarantee that any dish is completely free of any allergen. The steamed "diet menu" means no added fat, salt or sugar; it is not allergen-free.
